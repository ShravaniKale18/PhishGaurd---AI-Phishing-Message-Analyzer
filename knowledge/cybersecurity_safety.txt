If a message appears suspicious, do not provide passwords, OTPs,
banking credentials, or other sensitive information.

Do not rely only on the appearance of a message to determine whether
it is legitimate.

Verify unexpected requests independently using an organization's
official application or website.

Do not click suspicious links simply to investigate them.

If an account notification appears suspicious, open the official
service directly rather than following the link in the message.

Risk scores generated by automated systems are indicators rather
than absolute proof of malicious activity.
