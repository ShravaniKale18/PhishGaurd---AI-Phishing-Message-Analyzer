Phishing is a cyberattack technique where an attacker attempts to
deceive a person into revealing sensitive information or interacting
with malicious content.

Common phishing indicators include requests for passwords, account
verification, OTPs, banking information, payment information, or
other sensitive credentials.

Phishing messages frequently impersonate banks, online services,
delivery companies, government organizations, or popular websites.

Unexpected requests for sensitive information should be treated
with caution.

A legitimate organization generally should not require users to
share passwords or one-time passwords through an unsolicited message.

Common phishing categories include credential phishing, financial
phishing, account takeover attempts, prize scams, and impersonation.
